Use Your Phone

For the best handheld photobooth experience, open FRAMO directly in your mobile browser.

How it works:

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as this computer.
  2. Open your mobile browser (Safari / Chrome).
  3. Navigate to your local server address (e.g., http://[Your-IP]:5500).
  4. Enjoy handheld 4-cut photography with front and rear cameras!
